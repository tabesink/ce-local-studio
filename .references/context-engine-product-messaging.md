# Context Engine — Product Messaging

Terse, reusable copy for product users, executive sponsors, demos, and internal communications.

## One-line pitch

Context Engine turns governed organizational knowledge into fast, evidence-backed answers employees can trust.

## Elevator pitch

Context Engine is a secure internal knowledge workstation that lets employees ask questions across approved company sources and receive streamed answers grounded in traceable evidence. Administrators control what knowledge is available, how it is processed, and who can use it—without exposing sensitive infrastructure, credentials, or raw retrieval data.

## Executive pitch

Context Engine shortens the path from “where is the answer?” to “here is the answer—and the source.” It makes fragmented institutional knowledge easier to use while preserving access controls, provenance, operational accountability, and organizational ownership of the AI stack.

## The business value

- **Faster decisions:** Find answers across approved internal material without manually searching document silos.
- **Greater confidence:** Every grounded answer is tied to authorized evidence.
- **Lower knowledge friction:** Turn static source documents into an accessible, conversational resource.
- **Governed AI adoption:** Keep administrators in control of data boundaries, models, ingestion, and lifecycle.
- **Reduced operational risk:** Protect credentials, private identifiers, source storage, and runtime details behind the application boundary.
- **Durable organizational memory:** Preserve user-owned conversations for review, continuity, and reuse.

## Feature descriptions

### Evidence-grounded answers

Ask questions in natural language and receive answers synthesized from authorized company sources—not unsupported model knowledge.

### Traceable evidence

Inspect the evidence behind an answer, then open the governed source document at the nearest authorized location.

### Knowledge Domains

Organize sources into isolated, administrator-controlled knowledge boundaries so questions are answered from the right corpus.

### Streaming chat

See answers develop in real time, with clear retrieval, reconnect, cancellation, replay, and failure states.

### Durable conversations

Return to prior conversations and answers without repeating completed retrieval or model work.

### Governed document library

Browse approved source documents by Knowledge Domain, filter the library, and securely preview supported PDFs.

### Controlled ingestion

Administrators upload, prepare, index, retry, cancel, and remove source documents through governed workflows.

### Secure model configuration

Administrators manage model profiles, parser defaults, and write-only provider credentials without exposing secrets to users.

### Role-based administration

Separate member workflows from administrative controls while enforcing permissions on every authoritative backend request.

### Safe deletion and redaction

Removing a source or domain fences future retrieval and redacts affected answer content and evidence.

### Private deployment architecture

Keep retrieval runtimes, storage locations, provider details, credentials, and internal identifiers outside the browser trust boundary.

## Why Context Engine

- **Grounded by design:** No evidence means no fabricated domain answer.
- **Governed by design:** Every query stays within one authorized Knowledge Domain.
- **Private by design:** Sensitive infrastructure and retrieval internals remain private.
- **Traceable by design:** Answers connect users back to safe, authorized evidence.
- **Resilient by design:** Idempotency, leases, replay, and lifecycle fencing protect long-running work.

## User-facing product summary

Choose a Knowledge Domain, ask a question, and get a streamed answer grounded in approved sources. Review the supporting evidence, open the source document, and keep the conversation for later.

## Administrator-facing product summary

Configure trusted models and parsers, deploy isolated Knowledge Domains, govern source ingestion and indexing, and control the knowledge available for grounded answers.

## Suggested demo narrative

1. Select an approved Knowledge Domain.
2. Ask a real business question.
3. Watch the grounded answer stream.
4. Open the answer’s evidence.
5. Follow the evidence into the governed source document.
6. Show how an administrator controls domains, models, and source lifecycle.

Context Engine is a Phase 1 release candidate with the core governed RAG workstation implemented. Production acceptance, recovery drills, deployed-ingress validation, security review, and final browser/accessibility evidence are still in progress.
